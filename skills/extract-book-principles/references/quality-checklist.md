# Quality checklist

Complete every applicable check before delivery.

## Source and coverage

- [ ] Identify the exact edition, language, format, and processed scope.
- [ ] Base the synthesis on the source itself, not only its title, table of contents, reviews, or prior model knowledge.
- [ ] Disclose missing, unreadable, abridged, or selectively processed material.
- [ ] Keep the source and full-text derivatives private and out of the deliverable.

## Fidelity

- [ ] Make the central thesis and thought map reflect the whole processed scope.
- [ ] Resolve important claims to a real chapter, section, page, or honest coarse location.
- [ ] Mark AI inference and practical adaptation instead of attributing them to the author.
- [ ] Prefer paraphrase; keep quotations short, necessary, attributed, and non-substitutive.
- [ ] Preserve qualifications, counterexamples, and meaningful disagreements inside the book.

## Usefulness

- [ ] Keep principles distinct and few enough to remember.
- [ ] Make the highlights selective and more useful than a chapter list.
- [ ] Give each principle a mechanism or reason, not just a slogan.
- [ ] State misuse boundaries that would prevent an obvious bad application.
- [ ] Make practices concrete, low-risk, observable, and appropriate to the user's goal.
- [ ] Ensure AI memory sentences preserve causal meaning rather than oversimplifying it.

## Deliverable

- [ ] Produce one coherent `book-essence.md` unless the user requests another format.
- [ ] Keep engineering details out unless the user requests them or they explain a material limitation.
- [ ] Remove repetition, unsupported certainty, decorative quotes, and generic motivational filler.
- [ ] Label source-check status honestly; do not call AI-only extraction verified.
